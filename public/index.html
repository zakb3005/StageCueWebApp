ABOUT

WHAT IS STAGECUE?

StageCue is a free application/platform for creating and sharing collaborative stories with your friends and others. Simple, intuitive tools help bring your ideas center stage with up to 4 others either online or locally.

HOW IT WORKS

One user, the server host, will act as the story’s Director. The Director role has access to a list of Objects, Stages, and Scenes.

OBJECTS

Objects represent props, characters, other items that can be inserted into the scene. These objects can be freely moved around, mirrored, and deleted by the director. The size of an object is determined by the image's dimensions, roughly conforming to 16 bit pixel art.

STAGES

Stages are background images that can be swapped out by the director to represent different settings and locations for the scene to take place in.

SCENES

Scenes are combinations of objects and stages. A scene will load in a group of objects and their positions all at once. It also loads in the background image from its stage. This makes it easy to instantly swap between different scenes with various props, characters, and backgrounds without needing to place everything by hand.

THE ROLE OF THE OTHER PLAYERS

The rest of the participating users in a story are known as Actors. The Director role can assign any object in the scene to one of the Actors for them to take control of as a ‘player character’.

Actors can freely move their assigned object/character, they can create speech bubbles to make their character talk, and they have the unique ability to write out an ‘action’ as a verb or short description of how they want to interact with the scene.

HOW THE DIRECTOR AND ACTORS INTERACT

The Director and the Actors take turns doing whatever they want on their turn. The actors can make their character interact with other objects or characters in the scene through dialog or through specific actions. The director’s job is to then update the scene according to what the actor has done, whether that’s by moving an object, swapping an object out, making an non-player character speak back to the actor, or whatever else the director wants to do to contribute to the story themselves.

This back and forth between the director and actors is similar to the interaction between a DM (Dungeon Master) and the players of a Tabletop RPG such as D&D (Dungeons & Dragons). The director controls the world around the actors, and the actors interact with that world using their controlled characters.

Through this collaboration, StageCue users are able to create any story they can imagine together.

CREATING NEW OBJECTS, STAGES, AND SCENES

Assets Folder

When launching the StageCue application for the first time, a group of folders and assets will be automatically created in your computer’s Documents folder under the named, 'StageCue'. Any desired subfolders can be created within these 3 main folders to keep things organized.

To create Objects and Stages, simply drag any .png files into the folders of their corresponding type. That’s it, it’s all ready to go!

To create a new Scene, you must do so in-session as the director. By clicking the 'Save Scene' button while inside of the 'Scenes' folder in the editor’s browser, you can save the current state of the scene as a .json file, which can then be reinserted whenever you want.

Creating a new story in StageCue is best when it’s spontaneous and improvised, so it’s recommended to treat scenes as a way to set up an interesting scenario for the actors to expand upon naturally!
